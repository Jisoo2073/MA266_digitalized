Theorem.

\( F(s) = \mathcal{L}\{f(t)\}(s) \), then

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}\{e^{at} f(t)\} &= F(s-a) \\ \mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s-a)\} &= e^{at} f(t) \end{aligned} \]

Partial fraction decomposition

  1. \( (s-a)^n \) is a factor of denominator\[ \frac{A_1}{s-a} + \frac{A_2}{(s-a)^2} + \dots + \frac{A_n}{(s-a)^n} \]
  2. \( ((s-a)^2 + b^2)^n \) is a factor\[ \frac{A_1 s + B_1}{(s-a)^2 + b^2} + \frac{A_2 s + B_2}{[(s-a)^2 + b^2]^2} + \dots + \frac{A_n s + B_n}{[(s-a)^2 + b^2]^n} \]

Laplace transform

Function \( f(t) \) Laplace Transform \( \mathcal{L}\{f(t)\}(s) \)
\( e^{at} t^n \) \( \frac{n!}{(s-a)^{n+1}} \)
\( e^{at} \cos kt \) \( \frac{s-a}{(s-a)^2 + k^2} \)
\( e^{at} \sin kt \) \( \frac{k}{(s-a)^2 + k^2} \)

Inverse Laplace Transform Examples

eg 1.

\[\begin{aligned} F(s) &= \frac{4s+1}{s^2 - 8s + 41} = \frac{4s+1}{(s-4)^2 + 25} \\ &= \frac{4(s-4) + 17}{(s-4)^2 + 25} \\ &= 4\frac{s-4}{(s-4)^2 + 25} + \frac{17}{5}\frac{5}{(s-4)^2 + 25} \end{aligned}\]\[\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\}(t) = 4e^{4t}\cos 5t + \frac{17}{5}e^{4t}\sin 5t\]

eg 2.

\[\begin{aligned} F(s) &= \frac{3}{s^3 - 4s^2} = \frac{3}{s^2(s-4)} \\ &= \frac{A_1}{s} + \frac{A_2}{s^2} + \frac{B_1}{s-4} \end{aligned}\]\[A_1 s(s-4) + A_2(s-4) + B_1 s^2\]\[\begin{cases} A_1 + B_1 = 0 \\ -4A_1 + A_2 = 0 \\ -4A_2 = 3 \end{cases} \qquad \begin{aligned} B_1 &= \frac{3}{16} \\ A_1 &= -\frac{3}{16} \\ A_2 &= -\frac{3}{4} \end{aligned}\]\[\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\}(t) = -\frac{3}{16} - \frac{3}{4}t + \frac{3}{16}e^{4t}\]

An alternate way

\[\begin{aligned} F(s) &= \frac{1}{s}\left(\frac{3}{s(s-4)}\right) \\ &= \frac{1}{s}\frac{3}{4}\left(\frac{1}{s-4} - \frac{1}{s}\right) \end{aligned}\]\[\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{3}{4}\left(\frac{1}{s-4} - \frac{1}{s}\right)\right\} = \frac{3}{4}(e^{4t} - 1)\]

Inverse Laplace Transform (Continued)

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} &= \int_0^t \frac{3}{4}(e^{4u} - 1) \, du \\ &= \left. \left(\frac{3}{16}e^{4u} - \frac{3}{4}u\right) \right|_0^t \\ &= \frac{3}{16}e^{4t} - \frac{3}{4}t - \frac{3}{16} \end{aligned} \]
