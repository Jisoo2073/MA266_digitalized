Sec 7.1 Laplace transforms and inverse transforms

New theory: Laplace transform

Def. Given function \( f(t) \) defined for all \( t > 0 \)

\[ \begin{aligned} F(s) = \mathcal{L}\{f(t)\}(s) &= \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} f(t)\,dt \\ &= \lim_{b \to \infty} \int_{0}^{b} e^{-st} f(t)\,dt \end{aligned} \]

e.g.

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}\{1\}(s) = \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st}\,dt &= \lim_{b \to \infty} \left[ -\frac{1}{s} e^{-bs} + \frac{1}{s} \right] \\ &= \frac{1}{s} \end{aligned} \]

e.g.

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\}(s) = \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} e^{at}\,dt &= \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-(s-a)t}\,dt \\ &= \left[ -\frac{e^{-(s-a)t}}{s-a} \right]_{t=0}^{\infty} \end{aligned} \]

\( \mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\}(s) = \frac{1}{s-a} \) for \( s > a \)

Gamma function:

\[ \Gamma(x) = \int_0^\infty e^{-t} t^{x-1} \, dt \quad (x > 0) \]

Basic facts:

  • \( \Gamma(1) = 1 \)
  • \( \Gamma(x+1) = x\Gamma(x) \)
  • \( \Gamma(n+1) = n! \), \( n \) positive integer.

Theorem: \( f(t) = t^a \), \( a > -1 \)

\[ \mathcal{L}\{t^a\}(s) = \frac{\Gamma(a+1)}{s^{a+1}} \]

In particular \( \mathcal{L}\{t^n\}(s) = \frac{n!}{s^{n+1}}, \quad s > 0 \)

Unit step function:

Let \( a \ge 0 \)

\[ u_a(t) = \begin{cases} 0, & t < a \\ 1, & t \ge a \end{cases} \]

Theorem: \( \mathcal{L}\{u_a(t)\}(s) = \frac{e^{-as}}{s}, \quad s > 0 \)

Linearity:

\( \mathcal{L}\{af(t) + bg(t)\} = a\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\} + b\mathcal{L}\{g(t)\} \) \( a, b \) constants

Inverse Laplace transform

\( f(t) = \mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} \)

eg. \( \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{1}{s}\right\}(t) = 1 \)    \( \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{1}{s-a}\right\}(t) = e^{at} \)

\[ \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{\Gamma(a+1)}{s^{a+1}}\right\} = t^a \]

eg. Find inverse of \( F(s) = \frac{13}{4s+28} \)

\[ \mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\}(t) = \frac{13}{4}\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{1}{s+7}\right\}(t) = \frac{13}{4}e^{-7t} \]
